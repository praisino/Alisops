About

Built for teams who outgrew the spreadsheet they started with.

Alisops exists for anyone still stitching reports together by hand — a founder juggling five tabs, or a department running critical processes through tools that were never built to scale. We build the system once, so the manual work stops for good, whether that's one spreadsheet or a dozen workflows across a growing team.

The goal isn't a prettier spreadsheet. It's a system you never have to babysit.

Every tool we ship is designed to run quietly in the background — updating itself, flagging what matters, and staying out of your way until you need it.
