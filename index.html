How we protect your data at each step
Security & data governance

Your data is protected at every step, whatever the size of the job.

A five-person business still holds customer names, payment details and staff records. We apply the same controls to a small tracker as to an enterprise pipeline.

Before access

Agreed in writing

We sign an NDA, plus a data processing agreement where personal data is involved, and list exactly what we'll access and why.

During the build

Inside your accounts

We use named logins with multi-factor authentication. Your data stays in your systems, and we test on masked or sample data wherever we can.

At handover

Access closed off

We remove our access, rotate any shared passwords and API keys, and give you a written record of who can see what.

After

Easy to check

Access rules, logs and backups are documented so your team can review them, or we can review them as part of ongoing support.

Built into every system

  • Least accessEach person and each integration gets only the permissions it needs.
  • Less personal dataForms and reports collect and show only the fields the job requires.
  • Role-based viewsPeople see the rows and columns their role needs, nothing more.
  • Traceable changesVersion history and logs show who changed what, and when.
  • Backups firstWe copy your data before restructuring anything.
  • No secrets in chatPasswords and keys are shared through a password manager, never by email or message.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do we have to switch to new software?

Usually not. Most teams already have what they need in Excel and Microsoft 365, or Google Sheets and Workspace, plus a CRM, so that's where we build. When the data outgrows a spreadsheet, we move it to a database on Google Cloud or AWS and keep the reports where your team already looks, such as Power BI or Looker Studio.

Can you work with the spreadsheets we already have?

Yes, and most projects start there. Your current sheets show us how the business really runs, which is the best starting point for anything we build.

How do you keep our data secure?

We work inside your accounts with named logins and multi-factor authentication, take only the access the job needs, and remove it at handover. Everything is agreed in writing before we start. See the full process.

How is a project priced?

After the intro call and mapping, you get a written scope and a price before any build work starts. Ongoing support is a flat monthly retainer. Templates have their own pricing, and people on the waitlist get early-access rates.

How much of my team's time will it take?

Not much. We need the intro call, a walkthrough of how the work is done today and someone to test with real data before handover. We handle the rest.

What happens after handover?

The system is yours and comes with documentation. If you'd like us to keep maintaining and improving it, that's what fractional ops support is for.
